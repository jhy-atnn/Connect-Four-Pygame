# 🎮 Connect-Four-Pygame

Connect Four is a classic two-player strategy game built using Python and Pygame.

Players take turns dropping their colored discs into a 7-column, 6-row vertical grid. The goal is to be the first to connect **four of your own discs in a row**—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally—while also blocking your opponent from doing the same.

---

## ✨ Features

- 👥 **2-Player Gameplay** (Player vs Player)
- 🎯 Classic Connect Four mechanics
- 💣 **Bomb Feature**
  - Players can drop a bomb into a chosen column
  - The bomb clears or disrupts pieces in that column (depending on your logic)
  - Adds strategy and unpredictability to the game
- 🔊 **Sound Effects**
  - Piece drop sound
  - Bomb explosion sound
  - Win / game-over sound
- 🎨 Simple and clean Pygame interface

---

## 🧠 Objective

Be the first player to connect **four discs in a row** while strategically using bombs to disrupt your opponent’s progress.
